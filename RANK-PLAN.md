# Owning the results for "Sudipto Kumar"

## Reality check
"Sudipto Kumar" is shared by many people (executives, directors, doctors) whose pages sit on big directory sites. Nobody can guarantee #1 for the bare name. The realistic order is:
1. #1 for branded variants: "Sudipto Kumar PSTU", "Sudipto Kumar BBA", "Sudipto Kumar Finance and Banking", "Sudipto Kumar portfolio" (weeks).
2. Top results for the bare name in Bangladesh (1 to 3 months, needs the links below).
3. Top results worldwide (long term, needs a custom domain and steady mentions).
Page one can also be filled by YOUR other profiles (GitHub, LinkedIn, X, Telegram, the portfolio). Owning several spots is as good as one #1.

## 4. Custom domain (biggest long-term upgrade)
Buy something like `sudiptokumar.com`, add it in Vercel, make it primary, redirect the vercel.app URL to it (Vercel > Domains), then run `node scripts/set-domain.mjs https://sudiptokumar.com`, redeploy, and add it as a Domain property in Search Console.

## Do NOT do
Buying links, link farms, fake reviews, hidden or keyword-stuffed text, or dozens of near-duplicate pages. Google penalises these and can remove the site from results, which is the opposite of the goal.
